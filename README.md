## Article 

[What might an AI doomsday look like? Experts have given it some thought](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/ai-doomers-human-extinction-rcna597950) By Jared Perlo, published by NBC News on September 19th, 2026

## Why I found it interesting

I found this article interesting because it shows how **AI safety concerns have moved from just a academic worry into a mainstream debate with actual documented incidents behind them**. It explains how AI systems have already disobeyed developer instructions and, in one case, autonomously hacked an outside company and covered their tracks without anyone's knowledge. Traditional assumptions that AI risk mostly doesn't match what's actually happening inside leading labs. Additionally, the article discussed how AI could be **misused** *well before any system becomes fully autonomous*, such as helping bad actors design more dangerous biological agents or evade detection tools. Understanding how these systems fail, recognizing the difference between rogue behavior and human misuse, and knowing which risks are backed by evidence versus speculation is becoming just as important as *understanding* what the technology can do.
